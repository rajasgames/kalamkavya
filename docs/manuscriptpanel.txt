Manuscript Editor Panel
The Editor Panel serves as the central hub for creative production, providing a robust environment for hierarchical content organization. It facilitates a seamless transition from macro-level structural planning to micro-level scene development.
Hierarchical Organization
Chapters: High-level containers that define the major segments of the narrative arc.
Scenes: Granular writing units nested within chapters, allowing writers to focus on specific beats and character interactions.
Multi-Page Interface: Supports the creation of expansive drafts across multiple pages, ensuring that complex scenes remain manageable and focused.
Planner and Plot Board
The Plot Board is a visual strategic tool designed to map out the narrative journey. Each column represents a critical stage in storytelling, ensuring the manuscript maintains momentum and thematic resonance.
1. Setup
Establishes the ordinary world, introduces the protagonist, and provides the necessary context for the story to begin.
2. Inciting Incident
The catalyst that disrupts the protagonist's status quo and sets the primary conflict in motion.
3. Rising Action
A series of events and obstacles that build tension and develop the central conflict.
4. Midpoint
A significant shift in the story where the stakes are raised and the protagonist often moves from reactive to proactive.
5. Complications
Internal and external hurdles that test the protagonist's resolve and lead toward the climax.
6. Climax
The ultimate peak of tension and conflict where the protagonist faces the story's greatest challenge.
7. Falling Action
The aftermath of the climax, dealing with the immediate consequences of the resolution.
8. Resolution
The conclusion of the narrative arc where loose ends are tied and a new status quo is established.
Custom Columns
Provides flexibility for non-linear storytelling or specific tracking needs, such as subplots, world-building notes, or character arcs that run parallel to the main plot.
Outline Panel
The Outline Panel acts as a navigation and structural overview tool. It provides a condensed view of the entire project, allowing writers to jump between sections instantly and maintain a clear vision of the document's architecture.
